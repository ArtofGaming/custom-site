Warning: This game contains descriptions of abuse such as physical abuse, stalking, and controlling behavior.

Selene and Mara have been a couple for years. On the outside they look like your typical everyday couple. But in this short glimpse into their lives we see how unhealthy their dynamic is.

This was made for O2A2 2026, meaning it has less than 1000 words, 1 bg track, 1 sound effect, and 1 sprite. It's highly experimental for me, but a story I hope you enjoy all the same.
