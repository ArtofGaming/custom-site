Plot:

To many, the life of a paranormal exterminator seems exciting and glamorous. But for Aurora Nelson, a career woman who loves what she does and does it a bit too well? Well, she's burnt out, depressed, and spends way too much time doomscrolling with her cats. Whether she wants to admit it or not, Aurora's in search of a cure for her lonely heart.

That's where Nicole floats in: a calculating spirit with a penchant for possession who is soooooo over being dead. And she knows her ticket out—a warm body named Aurora.



Features:

A fully-voiced kinetic (no choices!) visual novel
A trans woman main character who DEFINITELY doesn't want to kiss a beautiful ghost
20k words of sweet, funny enemies-to-lovers romance
Lots of dirty jokes
Original character artwork by Corrocerous and simplyhalina
Soundtrack by jnsa
High-resolution, 4K scaled graphics
