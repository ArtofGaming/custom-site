# Spooktober Review
After finishing the first version of our Spooktober entry, Possessed By Fate, for Spooktober we've gotten a lot of comments complimenting all parts of the game. While my part didn't get specifically picked out in a lot of comments, that kinda means I did my job well haha. With camera and sprite movement often if they get picked out it's because it's giving people headaches, triggering motion sickness, etc. This has also been the game of mine that's gotten the most attention. Weirdly enough it's kinda made me think harder about how I present myself to the world and made me realize that a lot of stuff about promotion and getting eyes on your work. In the end, if you gush about your work enough, people will want to check it out! I have yet to play any other entries but a lot of fellow contestants have been playing each others games and that's been really cute! I got to play a few entries too! The game will be coming to Steam after we make a few needed updates to it which will make it technically my first Steam release!

But I got to play some games as well. As a result, I've felt super inspired! I got even more ideas for projects than I already had and got new inspiration for a remake I've been dissatisfied with my prior outline and first remake attempt for. I've decided I'm tired of the cycle of working on one project and neglecting my other ideas and then getting steered away from that idea because I feel guilty for ignoring the others. So that means another Cam's Cavalcade eventually, also a slow update to my "Currently Working On" section.
